How to merge audio files on iPhone

Short answer: in SoundPull, open Merge audio, add two or more audio files or videos, put them in order and tap Merge. You get a single MP3, M4A or WAV file, made on your iPhone.

Steps with SoundPull

  1. Open SoundPull and tap Merge audio at the bottom of the home screen.
  2. Add files with Choose Video (videos in your photo library) or From Files (videos or audio files, such as voice memos saved to Files). Add at least two.
  3. Set the order with the ↑ and ↓ buttons. The files are joined from top to bottom; ✕ takes one out.
  4. Under Output format, pick MP3, M4A or WAV. The total length of the merged audio is shown just below it.
  5. Tap Merge. When you see Audio merged, tap Save / Share.

You can mix videos and audio files in one list. From a video, only the sound is used.

Questions

Does SoundPull send my files to a server?
No. All processing is on-device. Your videos and audio never leave your iPhone.
Is SoundPull a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no ads. Every feature is included. The App Store shows the price for your country.
