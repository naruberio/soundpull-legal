How to extract audio from a video on iPhone

Short answer: open SoundPull, pick MP3, M4A or WAV, then choose the video. SoundPull takes the soundtrack out on your iPhone and lets you save it to Files or send it to another app. Nothing is uploaded.

Steps with SoundPull

  1. Open SoundPull. Under Output format, pick MP3, M4A or WAV.
  2. Tap Choose Video to pick from your photo library, or From Files for a video stored in the Files app.
  3. Wait while Extracting audio… is shown. Longer videos take longer.
  4. When you see Audio extracted, tap Save / Share and choose Save to Files or the app you want to send it to.

Only need part of the video? Turn on Trim a section before you choose the video, then set the start and end. If a video carries more than one audio track, for example two languages, SoundPull asks which one to use. Match the volume evens out audio that is too quiet or too loud.

For lots of clips at once, Batch extract at the bottom of the home screen lets you pick several videos and extracts them one after another.

Without another app: the Shortcuts method

The Shortcuts app that comes with iOS can do a basic version of this. A shortcut built around the Encode Media action, with the audio-only option turned on, saves the soundtrack as an M4A file. It costs nothing. The trade-offs: you build the shortcut yourself, and it saves M4A (or AIFF) but not MP3 or WAV.

If M4A is all you need and you are comfortable setting up a shortcut, that route is enough. SoundPull is for when you want MP3 or WAV, or want to pick, trim and save in a few taps without building anything.

Which format should I pick?

FormatGood for
M4A (AAC)Small files that sound good. Plays everywhere on Apple devices.
MP3Plays almost anywhere: older players, car stereos, websites that only take MP3.
WAVUncompressed audio for editing software. Files are much larger.

Questions

Does SoundPull send my videos to a server?
No. All processing is on-device. Your videos and audio never leave your iPhone.
Is SoundPull a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no ads. Every feature is included. The App Store shows the price for your country.
Can I use films I bought or videos inside streaming apps?
No. Protected content can't be used, and SoundPull does not bypass DRM. Use only videos you own or have the rights to.
What do I need?
An iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later.
