Privacy Policy — SoundPull

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This Privacy Policy describes how "SoundPull" (the "App") handles information about you when you use it. The App is published by naruberio ("we", "us", "our"). SoundPull extracts the audio track from videos you choose and saves it as an audio file (M4A, MP3, WAV, or M4R for ringtones). It can also export a copy of a video with the audio removed, and transcribe extracted audio to text using on-device speech recognition. Everything happens on your device.

1. Summary

We do not collect any personal data. Your videos, the audio we extract from them, and everything you do in the App stay on your device. We do not operate servers, and the App contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. The App does not require network access to work.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, or share any of the following:

  • Account information (email, name, profile) — the App has no accounts or sign-in
  • Your videos, the audio tracks we extract, or any file you import or export
  • Contacts, location, calendar, microphone, or camera data
  • Your photo library contents — see section 6
  • Device identifiers used for advertising or tracking
  • Crash reports or analytics events sent to third parties

3. How Your Videos and Audio Are Processed

Audio extraction happens entirely on your device using Apple's AVFoundation framework. When you select a video, the App reads it locally, writes the result — an audio file (M4A, MP3, WAV or M4R), or a copy of the video with its audio removed — to a temporary on-device location, and lets you preview, save, or share it.

Transcription (turning audio you have already extracted into text) uses Apple's Speech framework and is required to run on your device. If on-device recognition is not available for your language or device, the App tells you so and does not run the feature — it never falls back to sending audio to a server. Neither the audio nor the resulting text is sent to Apple or to us.

Your videos, the extracted audio, and any transcript are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Temporary working files are deleted once they are no longer needed: the imported copy of your video as soon as extraction finishes, and the extracted file when you start another extraction or discard the result. A copy created for the iOS share sheet is kept until the App is next launched, because the app you shared to may still be reading it; the App removes any leftover temporary files at the next launch. Anything you do not save is discarded.

4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following small pieces of data only on your device (using iOS local storage). This data never leaves your device.

DataPurposeStorage

5. Apple App Store

SoundPull is a paid app: you buy it once on the App Store and every feature is included. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising. The purchase is processed by Apple Inc. through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, payment card, or any other personal information from Apple. Apple's handling of your purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

The App requests the minimum permissions needed and only when you use the related feature:

PermissionWhy it is requested
Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription)So you can choose a video from your library to extract its audio. The App reads only the video you pick, and processing happens on your device. SoundPull does not scan, upload, or index your photo library.
Speech Recognition (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription)So you can transcribe audio you have already extracted. You are asked for this permission only when you start a transcription. Recognition is required to run on your device (requiresOnDeviceRecognition); if on-device recognition is unavailable, the App does not run the feature and never sends your audio to a server. Neither the audio nor the resulting text is sent to Apple or to us.

The App does not record from the microphone and does not use the camera or your location — speech recognition reads a file you already extracted, not live microphone input. The App does not request network access. App Tracking Transparency is never requested because the App does not track users.

7. Privacy Manifest

The App ships with an Apple Privacy Manifest declaring:

  • Tracking: None
  • Tracking domains: None
  • Collected data types: None
  • Required reason APIs used:
    • FileTimestamp (C617.1) — on-device file handling during extraction
    • DiskSpace (E174.1) — checking that there is enough free space before writing a file
    • UserDefaults (CA92.1) — storing the App's own settings on your device
    • SystemBootTime (35F9.1) — measuring how much time passes between events inside the App (timers)

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. Because we collect no personal data, the App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age threshold under any jurisdiction.

9. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, port, or delete. To delete all data stored by the App, uninstall it from your device.

For users in the EU (GDPR), the UK, California (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil (LGPD), or other privacy regimes: the App does not act as a controller or processor of your personal data, since none is collected by us.

10. International Use

The App is available globally. Because no data is transmitted off your device, no cross-border data transfer occurs.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above and by publishing the revised policy at the URL listed in section 12. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
