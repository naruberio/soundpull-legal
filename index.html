SoundPull

An iPhone app that pulls the sound out of your videos and works with audio files, all on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Save the soundtrack of a video, or just a trimmed part of it, as M4A, MP3 or WAV.
  • Convert audio files, including voice memos, to another format.
  • Merge several videos or audio files into one, or convert many in one go.
  • Make an iPhone ringtone (M4R) from a short clip.
  • Turn the speech in a recording into text.
